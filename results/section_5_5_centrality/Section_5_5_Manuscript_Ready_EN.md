### 5.5 Centrality in the Stablecoin Tail-Risk Network

Section 5.2 identifies coin-to-coin lower-tail links, but these links do not show which stablecoins occupy prominent network positions. For each of the 2,252 rolling 90-day windows, we use positive selected coefficients on reference-adjusted peg deviations to form a directed network from predictor to target. The five lagged macro-financial variables remain controls, not nodes. Outgoing and incoming strength measure positive coefficient weight per possible counterpart. Inward eigenvector centrality and outward harmonic closeness capture receiver prominence and network reach, respectively.

USDC has the highest mean outgoing strength (0.138; 90-day block-bootstrap 95% interval, 0.119–0.160), whereas BUSD has the highest incoming strength (0.150; 0.118–0.187). BUSD also leads in inward eigenvector centrality, while TUSD and USDC have the greatest outward harmonic closeness. Under the baseline specification, the date-level outgoing-minus-incoming contrast is positive for USDC (0.035; HAC(90), Holm-adjusted *p* = 0.047) and negative for BUSD (−0.072; *p* = 0.025) and GUSD (−0.027; *p* = 0.0058). The other eight contrasts do not survive correction. These aggregate node contrasts do not contradict the absence of significant individual pairwise direction differences in Section 5.2.

Full-sample ranks conceal changes in network position. Outgoing and incoming ranks correlate only 0.30 and 0.13 between the two chronological sample halves; the incoming rank correlation between scaled and unscaled specifications is −0.38. During the pre-specified SVB episode, USDC's median incoming strength rises from 0.107 in the preceding 30 days to 0.205 on 11–12 March 2023, exceeding its outgoing strength of 0.116 in the same two-day window. A separate check using Ethereum pool data shows that Curve 3pool imbalance increases from 0.060 to 0.302, its daily swap count from 269 to 11,510, and DAI/USDC Uniswap swap count from 332 to 2,454. These venue-specific data were not used to estimate the network. The network therefore records conditional positions that change with market state, not a stable hierarchy of causal transmitters or next-day predictors.
