# 5.6 statistical reporting audit

- **Question and estimand.** Incremental information from three predeclared, lagged venue-level on-chain variables, conditional on the Chapter 5.4 joint predictor set. A positive paired difference is the baseline metric minus the augmented metric.
- **Common sample.** 2022-04-02 to 2026-05-31, 1,521 UTC dates. There are 11 target equations per date in each of two timing specifications, giving 16,731 target-date observations per specification and 33,462 augmented fits in total. Input and response alignment is exact; the target response and scaling are identical between compared models. All fits and losses are finite (see `qa/`).
- **Analysis unit.** The date, after averaging across the eleven target coins. The 16,731 overlapping rolling equations per timing are not treated as independent observations. Bartlett-HAC(90) and 1,999 circular moving-block bootstrap draws with 90-day blocks address serial dependence due to overlapping training windows.
- **Multiplicity.** Four predeclared model contrasts (two timing specifications × selected GACV or held-out loss) form one Holm-adjusted family. The represented-three-versus-other-eight exploratory comparison uses a separate two-contrast Holm family.
- **Main pattern.** Selected GACV falls in both timing specifications, but the held-out differences are not distinguished from zero after correction. The fully lagged augmented model remains below the intercept-only benchmark in quantile skill. The full matched comparison does not establish forecasting gains.
- **Robustness of uncertainty.** Both 90-day block-bootstrap intervals for held-out loss differences cross zero, matching the HAC conclusion. Both selected-GACV block intervals exclude zero. Full-precision results are in `tables/Table_5_6_Matched_Model_Comparison.csv`.
- **Selection caveat.** Active-set frequencies and coefficient signs are descriptive outputs of a penalized, window-selected model. The analysis provides no post-selection coefficient confidence intervals or causal effects.
- **Data boundary.** Curve 3pool and the Uniswap USDC/USDT 0.01% pool are selective Ethereum venues involving DAI, USDC and USDT; they are not whole-market or coin-specific order books. The ±25-tick inventory measure is a price-centered AMM inventory proxy, not a 25 bp executable spread/depth. All three variables are dated at the previous UTC close; no on-chain history is backfilled or set to zero.
- **Index boundary.** An auxiliary mean-selected-lambda series can be computed on this subperiod, but adding predictors changes the penalty path. Absolute lambda levels across the 15- and 18-predictor models are not identical economic units. The frozen Chapter 4 DPI is retained as the primary index.
- **Remaining reviewer risk.** The extension uses only three additional features, rather than a broad microstructure panel. It tests marginal value of the accessible data; it does not establish that no microstructure data could improve the model. The five macro factors and 11-coin coverage remain those of the original thesis.
