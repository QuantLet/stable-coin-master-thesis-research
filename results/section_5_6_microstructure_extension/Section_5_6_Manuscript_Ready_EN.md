### 5.6 Additional On-Chain Information

The preceding analysis uses peg deviations and macro-financial variables. To assess whether trading conditions add information, we extend the joint model with three one-day-lagged indicators: Curve 3pool inventory imbalance, its log daily swap count, and log near-price inventory in the Uniswap v3 USDC/USDT pool. The comparison uses the same 11 target coins, 90-day windows and 5% Quantile-Lasso specification as Section 5.4. The common evaluation sample contains 1,521 dates from 2 April 2022 to 31 May 2026. We compare the original 15-predictor model with the 18-predictor extension on these identical dates.

At least one on-chain variable enters 90.6% of the same-day conditional equations and 93.2% of the fully lagged equations. Mean selected GACV falls by 4.5% and 7.5%, respectively (HAC(90), Holm-adjusted *p* < 0.001 and *p* = 0.0014). The held-out results are different: check loss rises by 1.1% in the same-day conditional comparison and falls by 2.2% when all predictors are lagged. Neither difference is distinguishable from zero after correction (*p* = 0.534 and 0.426). The augmented models also remain worse than the rolling intercept-only benchmark, with quantile skill scores of −25.5% and −34.4%. Frequent selection and better in-window fit therefore do not establish additional forecast value.
