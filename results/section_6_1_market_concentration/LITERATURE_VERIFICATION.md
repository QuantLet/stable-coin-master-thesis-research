# Literature verification for Section 6.1

Only sources cited in the section text are retained in the section-level BibTeX file.

1. Mayer, F. and Bofinger, P. (2024), *Financial Innovation*, 10, 93. DOI: `10.1186/s40854-023-00600-4`. The article uses concentration ratios and Herfindahl indices to study competition in cryptocurrency submarkets and discusses the market-capitalization position of major stablecoins. Publisher record: <https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40854-023-00600-4>.
2. Kosse, A., Glowka, M., Mattei, I. and Rice, T. (2023), BIS Papers No. 141. The report studies the evolution and structure of the stablecoin market and documents its dominance by a small number of large tokens. Official record: <https://www.bis.org/publications/paper-141-will-real-stablecoin-please-stand-up>.

These references motivate the choice of market-share concentration measures. All numerical results reported for Section 6.1 are newly calculated from the thesis sample and are not taken from either source.
