**Table 6.3 | Portfolio strategies and ex ante evaluation protocol.**

| Strategy | Objective | Information set | Constraints |
|---|---|---|---|
| EW | Equal allocation across the eleven-coin sample | Fixed universe | Long-only; weights sum to one; 30% maximum per coin |
| GMV | Minimize trailing reference-adjusted return variance | Trailing 90 days through t-1 | Long-only; weights sum to one; 30% maximum per coin |
| LTEC | Minimize lagged coin-specific DPI lambda exposure | Lambda estimated through t-1 | Long-only; weights sum to one; 30% maximum per coin |
| QTEC | Minimize an equal-weighted combination of lambda covariance and lagged lambda exposure | Trailing 90-day lambda covariance and lambda through t-1 | Long-only; weights sum to one; 30% maximum per coin |
