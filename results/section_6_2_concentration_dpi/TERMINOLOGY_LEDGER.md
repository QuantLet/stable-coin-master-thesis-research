# Terminology ledger

| Canonical term | Definition / use |
|---|---|
| Depegging Pressure Index (DPI) | Primary robust-scaled, strict minimum-GACV index defined in Chapter 4 |
| market-capitalization concentration | Concentration of the eleven-coin sample measured from positive observed USD market capitalization |
| normalized Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (NHHI) | HHI normalized for the number of active coins on each date |
| Top-2 market share | Combined capitalization share of the two largest observed stablecoins |
| Newey-West HAC(90) | Bartlett-kernel heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-consistent covariance with lag 90 |
| additional on-chain controls | Lagged Curve 3pool imbalance, Curve swap count and Uniswap USDC/USDT near-price inventory |
| structural exposure | Market concentration as a constraint on diversification, not a causal or daily risk indicator |
