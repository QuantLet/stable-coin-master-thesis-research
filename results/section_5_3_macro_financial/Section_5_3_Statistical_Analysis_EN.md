### Statistical analysis for Section 5.3

The analysis used the frozen coefficient panel from the primary 90-day, 5% Quantile-Lasso specification. A coefficient was classified as active when its absolute value exceeded $10^{-10}$. For each macro-financial factor and date, active, positive and negative indicators were averaged across the 11 target stablecoins; calendar date was therefore the sampling unit ($n=2,252$). Mean selection frequencies, sign balances and normalized absolute coefficient-weight shares were reported with Newey-West heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-consistent covariance using a Bartlett kernel and lag 90. Equality of the five factor-level selection frequencies was assessed with a four-degree-of-freedom HAC Wald test. The ten paired factor contrasts were Holm-adjusted as one comparison family. Target heterogeneity was tested separately for each factor using a ten-degree-of-freedom HAC Wald test, with Holm adjustment across the five factors. Factor-level positive-minus-negative sign tests formed a separate five-test Holm family. Robustness was assessed using the screened-GACV coefficient panel, the unscaled strict panel, exclusion of the 539 windows containing past-filled deviation cells, and chronological half-sample rank comparisons. Standardized coefficient weights were calculated within each target-date equation and are interpreted as descriptive coefficient mass rather than incremental predictive contribution.
