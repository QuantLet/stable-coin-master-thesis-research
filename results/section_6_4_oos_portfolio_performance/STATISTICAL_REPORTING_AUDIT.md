# Statistical reporting audit

- Analysis set: 2,162 common daily observations, 30 June 2020 to 31 May 2026.
- Primary endpoints: annualised volatility and 5% Expected Shortfall.
- Primary comparison family: GMV, LTEC and QTEC versus EW across two endpoints (six tests).
- Dependence handling: paired moving-block bootstrap with 1,999 replications and 90-day blocks.
- Multiplicity control: Holm adjustment across the six primary tests.
- Effect reporting: absolute paired differences, 95% bootstrap intervals, adjusted p values and risk ratios.
- Secondary outcome: maximum drawdown; reported descriptively and excluded from the primary multiplicity family.
- Cost analysis: common 10-basis-point one-way proportional scenario, not observed execution costs.
- Confirmatory conclusion: GMV improves both primary endpoints after Holm correction; LTEC and QTEC do not.
- Interpretive boundary: results support covariance-based diversification, not a claim that DPI lambda is a stable one-step-ahead trading signal. They do not negate the DPI's role as a contemporaneous systemic-risk measure.
