### 5.2 Stablecoin-to-Stablecoin Tail-Risk Links

This section isolates the 247,720 coin-to-coin coefficients from the primary Quantile-Lasso system. For target stablecoin \(j\), the coefficient on stablecoin \(i\) defines the conditional direction \(i\rightarrow j\). Since deviations are measured as price relative to the relevant reference asset minus one, a positive coefficient is a downside-aligned link: a lower deviation of \(i\) is associated with a lower conditional 5% quantile of \(j\). A negative coefficient denotes an inverse link. Each equation retains the other stablecoins and five lagged macro-financial controls. Statistical inference treats each calendar date as one sampling unit. The system-wide sign contrast and two taxonomy contrasts form one three-test Holm family; pair-specific directional differences form a separate family of 55 tests.

Across the 2,252 rolling windows, 63.5% of pair-window coefficients are active (HAC 95% CI, 62.3-64.6%). Downside-aligned links account for 39.6%, compared with 23.9% for inverse links. The 15.6-percentage-point difference is statistically distinguishable from zero (HAC(90) 95% CI, 11.6-19.7 percentage points; Holm-adjusted \(p=9.04\times10^{-14}\)). The selected network therefore exhibits a system-wide tilt towards common lower-tail movements.

The largest full-sample aligned frequencies are USDT→TUSD at 71.4% (block-bootstrap 95% CI, 61.6-81.3%), DAI→USDT at 67.5% (53.7-79.6%) and USDP→TUSD at 65.6% (51.9-78.5%). These rankings do not establish stable sender-receiver roles. Although the mean absolute difference between opposite directions is 5.9 percentage points, none of the 55 pair-specific directional differences remains statistically distinguishable from zero after HAC(90) and Holm correction. The aggregate result thus concerns the sign composition of the network, not a persistent bilateral transmission hierarchy.

The group-level structure is also statistically supported. Aligned selection is 9.1 percentage points more frequent for pairs sharing a reference asset than for pairs with different references (HAC(90) 95% CI, 7.1-11.0 percentage points; Holm-adjusted \(p=6.70\times10^{-20}\)). It is 4.4 percentage points more frequent within than across peg-support categories (1.7-7.1 percentage points; Holm-adjusted \(p=0.00144\)). These contrasts are calculated from one cross-sectional difference per date and remain associations after reference adjustment; they do not identify a trading, redemption or collateral transmission mechanism.

Robustness checks qualify the full-sample rankings. Strict and screened GACV rules produce effectively identical aligned-link ranks (Spearman \(\rho=1.000\)), and excluding the 539 windows containing past-filled deviation cells leaves the ranking similar (\(\rho=0.959\)). By contrast, aligned-frequency ranks correlate only 0.286 between robust-scaled and unscaled estimates. The two chronological sample halves correlate 0.169 and share only two of their ten highest-ranked links. Finally, aligned-link density differs by -1.2 percentage points between the severe and very-low DPI states (90-day block-bootstrap 95% CI, -3.9 to 1.7 percentage points). Together with the decline in total stablecoin inclusion reported in Section 5.1, this result indicates that network contraction at high DPI is concentrated in inverse links, not in a uniform loss of downside-aligned links. The evidence therefore supports a conditional network whose composition varies across specifications and periods, rather than a fixed hierarchy or a uniform expansion of coin-to-coin dependence when DPI is high.

Taken together, the selected coin-to-coin network exhibits significant system-wide downside alignment and reference- and design-based clustering, but no fixed bilateral transmission hierarchy. The links are conditional rather than causal: stablecoin regressors are contemporaneous, coefficients are penalised, and correlated predictors may substitute for one another. Section 5.3 next examines macro-financial associations before Section 5.4 compares internal and external components directly.
