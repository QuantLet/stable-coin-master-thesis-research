### Statistical analysis for Section 5.2

The calendar date, rather than the individual pair-window coefficient, was treated as the sampling unit. Network-density and contrast uncertainty was estimated using heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-consistent covariance with a Bartlett lag of 90 days, matching the estimation-window length. The system-wide aligned-minus-inverse contrast and the reference-asset and peg-support contrasts were each formed as one cross-sectional difference per date; their two-sided p values were adjusted jointly as a three-test family using Holm's method. Pair-specific directional asymmetry was tested separately for all 55 unordered stablecoin pairs, with Holm adjustment across that complete family. Pointwise intervals for link persistence and DPI-state contrasts were obtained from 1,999 circular moving-block bootstrap draws with 90-day blocks. Robustness analyses compared strict and screened GACV rules, robust-scaled and unscaled inputs, the full and imputation-unexposed samples, and two chronological sample halves. These procedures quantify uncertainty in the saved rolling selection sequences; they do not provide post-selection confidence intervals for the penalised regression coefficients.
