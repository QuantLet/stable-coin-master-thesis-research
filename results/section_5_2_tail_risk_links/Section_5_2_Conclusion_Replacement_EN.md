### Replacement conclusion for Section 5.2

Taken together, the selected coin-to-coin network exhibits significant system-wide downside alignment and reference- and design-based clustering, but no fixed bilateral transmission hierarchy. Downside-aligned links are substantially more prevalent than inverse links, and their incidence is higher among stablecoins sharing a reference asset or peg-support category. However, pair-specific direction differences are not statistically stable after dependence and multiplicity adjustment, while edge rankings vary across scaling choices and sample periods. The links should therefore be interpreted as state-dependent conditional associations rather than causal spillovers. Section 5.3 next examines macro-financial associations before Section 5.4 compares internal and external components directly.
